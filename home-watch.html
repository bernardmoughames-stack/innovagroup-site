The offer

Watched over while you’re away

A villa that stands empty still lives: AC cycles, water moves, dust settles, deliveries arrive. Small issues become expensive ones only when nobody is there to notice.

Home Watch puts a trained eye inside your property on a schedule you set — every visit documented with photographs, every issue flagged before it grows, every arrival prepared for. It is run by the same team that maintains the property, so what we find, we can also fix.

The service runs today across Dubai. Tell us about your property and how you use it, and we will build the visit routine around it.

What it covers

A presence, not just a key holder

The visit routine is agreed around your property and your travel pattern.

  • Scheduled interior and exterior property checks
  • Photographic report after every visit
  • AC, humidity, water and electrical spot checks
  • Arrival and guest preparation — cooling, lighting, provisioning
  • Post, deliveries and package handling
  • Plant care and watering
  • Pet feeding and care on your routine
  • Vendor and contractor access supervision
  • Housekeeping and linen coordination
  • Vehicle checks and battery care
  • Emergency response coordination with our 24/7 line
  • Direct line to the maintenance team when something needs fixing

Questions

Before you ask

How quickly can visits start?

Usually within a week of the walkthrough. Tell us your travel dates and we will schedule the first visit around them.

Is this the same as a maintenance contract?

It complements one. Maintenance keeps the systems serviced; Home Watch keeps eyes on the property between services — and the two are run by the same team, so nothing falls between them.

Can you look after pets and plants?

Yes. Pet feeding, plant watering and mail handling are part of the visit routine, on the schedule you set. We speak English, German and Arabic.

What happens in an emergency?

The visit team is backed by our 24/7 maintenance line. Anything affecting safety, security or habitability is escalated immediately and coordinated until it is resolved.
