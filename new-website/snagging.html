اطّلع على الباقات استفسر

The offer

The last moment your leverage is free

Once you accept handover, defects become a warranty claim: slower, more contested and sometimes at your cost. Before you accept, they are simply the developer's outstanding work.

A snagging inspection uses that window properly. We go through the property room by room against finishing standards, record every defect with a photograph and a location, grade it by severity, and issue a register formatted for the developer's own rectification process.

We are independent of the developer and the contractor. We are paid to find things.

فحص السناجينج يستثمر تلك النافذة كما ينبغي. نمرّ على العقار غرفةً بغرفة وفق معايير التشطيب، نوثّق كل عيبٍ بصورةٍ وموقع، نصنّفه حسب الخطورة، ونصدر سجلاً بصيغةٍ تناسب عملية الإصلاح لدى المطوّر نفسه.

What we inspect

Room by room, system by system

The inspection depth depends on the package; the reporting standard does not change.

  • Doors, windows, ironmongery, alignment and seals
  • Joinery, wardrobes, kitchen units and worktops
  • Tiling, grouting, flooring, skirting and transitions
  • Paint, plaster, ceilings and decorative finishes
  • Electrical points, switches, lighting and distribution boards
  • Plumbing fixtures, water pressure, drainage and leaks
  • Air conditioning performance, airflow and condensate
  • Thermal imaging of AC, wet areas and insulation gaps
  • Moisture readings on walls, floors and wet areas
  • Balconies, external finishes, roofs and drainage falls
  • Pool plant, landscape and external services where present
  • Severity-graded register with photographs and locations

How it works

From first call to close-out

  1. 01

    Book

    Ideally before you sign the handover acceptance. We can usually attend within a few days.

  2. 02

    Inspect

    A methodical pass through every room and system, photographing and locating each defect.

  3. 03

    Report

    A severity-graded register issued within 48 hours, formatted so the developer can work from it directly.

  4. 04

    Re-inspect

    After rectification we return, verify each item and issue a close-out report confirming what was actually fixed.

Questions

Before you ask

When should the inspection happen?

Between the developer notifying you that the unit is ready and you signing the handover acceptance. That is the point at which outstanding work is still clearly the developer's obligation.

What if the developer disputes the report?

Every item is photographed, located and graded, which makes most items difficult to dispute. Where a disagreement remains, we can attend the walkthrough and present the finding directly.

Can you snag a property that's already handed over?

Yes. It is still useful within the defects liability period, and essential before a fit-out so that pre-existing defects are recorded before your own contractor starts.

Do you carry out the repairs?

Not on a property we have snagged for you — the inspection stays independent. If you want the work done, our contracting and maintenance teams can quote separately once the register is closed.

الأسئلة

قبل أن تسأل

متى ينبغي أن يتم الفحص؟

بين إخطار المطوّر لك بجاهزية الوحدة وتوقيعك على قبول الاستلام. عند تلك النقطة تبقى الأعمال المتبقية التزاماً واضحاً على المطوّر.

ماذا لو اعترض المطوّر على التقرير؟

كل بندٍ مصوَّر ومحدَّد الموقع ومصنَّف الخطورة، ما يجعل الاعتراض على معظم البنود صعباً. وحين يبقى خلاف، يمكننا حضور الجولة وعرض النتيجة مباشرة.

هل يمكن فحص عقارٍ استُلم بالفعل؟

نعم. يبقى الفحص مفيداً خلال فترة ضمان العيوب، وضرورياً قبل أي تشطيب حتى تُوثَّق العيوب القائمة قبل أن يبدأ مقاولك عمله.

هل تنفّذون الإصلاحات؟

ليس في عقارٍ فحصناه لك — فالفحص يبقى مستقلاً. وإن أردت تنفيذ الأعمال، يمكن لفريقَي المقاولات والصيانة لدينا تقديم عرض سعرٍ منفصل بعد إقفال السجل.
