Privacy policy

What we collect through this website, why, who handles it for us, and how to ask us to delete it.

Last updated 18 November 2026

Who we are

innovagroup.co.ae is the website of Innova Group LLC, Meydan, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. For anything in this policy, write to info@innovagroup.co.ae or call +971 50 509 7758.

What we collect

  • When you send an enquiry: your name, your phone number, your email address if you give one, the service you choose, your message, and the language of the page you sent it from.
  • When you call, email or message us on WhatsApp: whatever you choose to send through those services.
  • When you browse: Google Analytics records the pages you visit, your approximate location, your device and browser, and how you arrived, using cookies.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, visit your property if you ask us to, prepare a quote, and carry out the work we agree. Analytics shows us which pages and adverts lead to enquiries, so we can improve the site and stop paying for adverts that don’t work. We don’t sell your details, and we don’t share them with anyone to market to you.

Who processes it for us

  • Web3Forms delivers the enquiry form to our email.
  • Google runs Google Analytics and serves the website’s fonts (Google Fonts), which shares your IP address with Google.
  • GitHub hosts the website and keeps standard server logs.

What stays in your browser

The site remembers your language and your choice of light or dark mode in your own browser. Apart from the Google Analytics cookies, we store nothing else there.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to deal with your request and any work that follows, and to keep the business records the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to info@innovagroup.co.ae. To stop Google Analytics, use your browser’s privacy settings or Google’s opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
