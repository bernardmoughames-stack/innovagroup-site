Snagging

Independent pre-handover inspection with a photographic, room-by-room defect report your developer can act on.

The last moment your leverage is free

Once you accept handover, defects become a warranty claim: slower, more contested and sometimes at your cost. Before you accept, they are simply the developer’s outstanding work.

We inspect room by room against finishing standards, record every defect with a photograph and a location, grade it by severity, and issue a register formatted for the developer’s own process. We are paid to find things.

What we do

Room-by-room inspection · Thermal imaging & moisture readings · MEP performance testing · Severity-graded photo register · Developer-ready 48h report · Re-inspection & close-out
