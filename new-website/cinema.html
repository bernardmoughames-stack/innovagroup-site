The offer

The room matters more than the equipment

Expensive speakers in an untreated room sound worse than modest speakers in a treated one. Most disappointing cinemas are a room problem wearing an equipment problem's clothes.

We start with the space: dimensions, reflections, seating distance, sightlines and where the noise is coming from. Only then do we specify equipment, and we specify it to suit the room rather than a price list.

Every installation is calibrated on completion — picture and sound measured and adjusted, not left at factory defaults — and handed over with a control system simple enough that guests can use it.

العرض

الغرفة أهم من المعدّات

سماعات باهظة في غرفةٍ غير معالجة تبدو أسوأ من سماعات متواضعة في غرفةٍ معالجة. معظم الصالات المخيّبة مشكلة غرفة ترتدي ثياب مشكلة معدّات.

نبدأ من المساحة: الأبعاد والانعكاسات ومسافة الجلوس وخطوط الرؤية ومصادر الضوضاء. بعدها فقط نحدد المعدّات — ونحددها لتناسب الغرفة، لا قائمة الأسعار.

كل تركيبٍ يُعايَر عند الإنجاز — صورةً وصوتاً، قياساً وضبطاً، لا تركاً على إعدادات المصنع — ويُسلَّم مع نظام تحكمٍ من البساطة بحيث يستطيع الضيوف استخدامه.

Scope of work

From acoustic model to first screening

Delivered as a standalone project or integrated into a wider fit-out by our contracting team.

Questions

Before you ask

Can you work alongside our interior designer?

Yes, and it usually produces a better room. We provide acoustic and technical requirements early so the designer can absorb them into the finishes rather than work around them afterwards.

Do we buy the equipment, or do you?

Either. We can supply and install as part of the package, or specify the schedule and install equipment you procure yourself.

Will you service a system you didn't install?

In most cases, yes. We start with a system audit and a calibration check, then propose a service agreement based on what we find.
