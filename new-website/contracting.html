The offer

The whole build, under one signature

Splitting a fit-out across a designer, a main contractor, an MEP subcontractor and an AV specialist means four programmes, four invoices and four versions of whose fault it is.

Turnkey contracting removes that. We take responsibility for design coordination, procurement, construction, services, finishes and commissioning — and for the sequence that holds them together. One programme, one cost plan, one project lead who answers the phone.

We work across residential villas and apartments, offices and clinics, retail and F&B units, and hospitality spaces. Scope can be fixed-price against a detailed bill of quantities, or open-book where the design is still moving.

Scope of work

What sits inside a turnkey contract

A typical engagement covers all of the following. Anything you want to keep in-house, we exclude in writing before we start.

  • Site survey, measured drawings and existing-condition report
  • Design coordination, technical drawings and value engineering
  • Authority submissions, permits and NOCs
  • Civil works, partitions, ceilings and structural alterations
  • MEP — electrical, HVAC, plumbing, drainage and fire systems
  • Joinery, wardrobes, kitchens and specialist finishes
  • Flooring, tiling, paint and decorative surfaces
  • AV, low-current, networking and smart-home integration
  • FF&E procurement, delivery and installation
  • Testing, commissioning and authority inspections
  • Independent snagging and rectification before handover
  • As-built drawings, O&M manuals, warranties and asset list

How it works

From first call to close-out

  1. 01

    Survey

    We measure the space, record existing conditions and confirm what the building and authority will allow.

  2. 02

    Price

    A detailed bill of quantities, a programme with milestone dates, and a written list of exclusions.

  3. 03

    Build

    One site team, sequenced trades, weekly progress reporting and photographic records throughout.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Testing, commissioning, independent snagging, rectification, then documentation and keys.

Questions

Before you ask

Can you work in an occupied building?

Yes. We regularly phase works or run out-of-hours programmes in occupied offices, retail units and residential buildings, coordinating with building management on access, noise windows and service isolations.

Who handles the authority approvals?

We do. Submissions, permits and NOCs are part of the Turnkey Complete and Design & Build Plus packages, and available as an add-on to Fit-Out Essential.

Can you take over a project another contractor started?

In most cases, yes — subject to a condition survey and a written record of what was completed and to what standard before we mobilise.

Is the price fixed?

Fixed-price against an agreed bill of quantities is our default. Where the design is still developing we can work open-book with an agreed management fee, converting to fixed price once drawings are frozen.

هل تعملون في مبنى مأهول؟

نعم. كثيراً ما ننفّذ الأعمال على مراحل أو خارج ساعات الدوام في مكاتب ووحدات تجزئة ومبانٍ سكنية مأهولة، بالتنسيق مع إدارة المبنى حول الدخول ونوافذ الضجيج وعزل الخدمات.

من يتولى موافقات الجهات الرسمية؟

نحن. التقديمات والتصاريح وشهادات عدم الممانعة جزء من باقتَي «تسليم مفتاح كامل» و«تصميم وبناء بلس»، ومتاحة كإضافة على باقة «أساسي للتشطيب».

هل يمكنكم استكمال مشروع بدأه مقاول آخر؟

في معظم الحالات نعم — شرط إجراء مسحٍ للحالة وتوثيقٍ مكتوب لما أُنجز وبأي معيار قبل تحرّكنا.

هل السعر ثابت؟

السعر الثابت وفق جدول كميات متفق عليه هو الأصل عندنا. وحين يكون التصميم لا يزال يتطوّر، نعمل بنظام الدفاتر المفتوحة بأتعاب إدارة متفق عليها، ثم نتحوّل إلى سعرٍ ثابت فور تثبيت المخططات.
